Who Are We

ARYCT (Active Retired York Catholic Teachers) is a community of retirees from the York Catholic District School Board who get together throughout the year to stay connected, active, and involved. We meet for guest speakers, outings, lunches and hands-on activities like our milk bag weavers group.

We're also proud to give back. Through member donations and fundraising, ARYCT has supported organizations like City Street Outreach, St. Vincent de Paul, the Salvation Army, Ride for Cancer, and local refugee and outreach programs while our Fill the Truck drives and hand-knitted donations have reached families and shelters across the region.

Whether it's an activity, a day trip, a themed tour, or simply lunch with old friends, ARYCT is about keeping the relationships and community spirit of our teaching years alive in retirement.

Melanie Fintelman, Kelly Pickard-Lefterys, Anna Santarossa, Sharon Gusz (co-chair), Ivana Todd (co-chair) and Marilynne Leach
