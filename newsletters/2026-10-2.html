  • Travel insurance, with up to $10 million in emergency medical coverage per person

Educators Financial Group

Alana Gould from Educators Financial Group also spoke at our gathering on October 15th. She reminded attendees that EFG does not work on commission and does not charge fees for consultations. Their focus is for you as a client to be educated and informed.

Financial specialists are available to meet with you via Zoom, in your home, or in their office. In addition to assisting you with your financial portfolio, they can advise on tax implications of various transactions and implications of your portfolio being passed onto a beneficiary. They will also assist those that are acting as executor for others.

Together for Publicly Funded Education Rallies

On Saturday, October 24, teachers and education workers from across Ontario will come together for five regional Together for Publicly Funded Education rallies. The Toronto Rally will be at Queen's Park.

Every Catholic teacher is strongly encouraged to make plans to attend the rally closest to them. Please also encourage a colleague from work, as well as your family, friends, neighbours, and other supporters of publicly funded education, to do the same.

Other Items That May Interest You

For the past 27 years, the American Lung Association (ALA) has released an annual "State of Our Air" report. The 2026 report found that nearly half of children in the U.S. live in places with unhealthy levels of air pollution. How does unhealthy air quality impact our children and the most vulnerable in our nation? How should we as people of faith respond? How different are things in Canada?
