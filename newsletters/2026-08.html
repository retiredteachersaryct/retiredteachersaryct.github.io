Fill the Truck: Back to School Drive

Wednesday, August 26, 10:00 – 11:00 a.m.
Our Lady Queen of the World Church Parking Lot
10411 Bayview Ave, Richmond Hill

Highest needs: backpacks and lunch bags.

It is very helpful to purchase a larger quantity of one or two items, rather than attempting to "fill a backpack," as items will be sorted for parents to select what is needed and most appropriate for their child/children.

Gently used clothing, linens, toys and small household items are also welcome! Packaging clothing in clear plastic bags is preferred with a visible label identifying sex and size.

For those that wish to contribute and are unable to attend, we accept e-transfers and will purchase for you! We will also provide you with an income tax receipt after the completion of the drive for donations over $25.00 with itemized receipts.

Donations to The Cycle of Care will also be accepted at the Back to School Drive.
